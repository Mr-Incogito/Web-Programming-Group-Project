Welcome to Knox College

Knox College is a secondary educational institution located in Spaldings, Clarendon, Jamaica. The school provides an environment where students are encouraged to develop academically, socially and personally. Through education, discipline and dedication, Knox College aims to prepare students to become responsible members of society.

Our Institution

Students are encouraged to develop discipline, responsibility, confidence and leadership skills.

Community Values
